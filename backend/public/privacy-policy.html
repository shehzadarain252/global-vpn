Privacy Policy — Global VPN

Last updated: November 2026

What Global VPN is

Global VPN is a free VPN app. It routes your internet traffic through WireGuard servers so that websites see the server's IP address and country instead of yours. The app is 100% free — there is no premium plan and no in-app purchases. It is funded by ads and by the credit system.

What we collect

  • Guest ID: a random identifier created on your device. No name, email or phone number is required to use the app.
  • Credit wallet: your credit balance and credit transactions, so the credit system works (welcome credits, VPN usage, rewarded-ad earnings).
  • Anonymous analytics (Firebase Analytics): anonymous usage statistics, for example how many people open the app and which countries are selected. No personal data is included.
  • Anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): crash data used only to fix bugs. No personal data is included.
  • Ads (Google AdMob): AdMob may collect advertising identifiers to show ads, in line with Google's privacy policy.

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your browsing history, your real name, email address, phone number, passwords, or the content of your internet traffic. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Important limits — please read

  • A VPN changes your IP address and apparent country. It does not change your GPS location or your SIM / mobile-network location.
  • No VPN can promise complete anonymity. Your internet provider still sees that you connect to a VPN server, and websites you log in to still know who you are.
  • Traffic is encrypted between your device and the VPN server. After the server, normal internet rules apply.

Your data and your choices

You can delete your guest account, wallet and transaction history at any time from the app: Settings → Delete Account. Deletion is permanent.

Children

Global VPN is not directed at children under 13.

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
